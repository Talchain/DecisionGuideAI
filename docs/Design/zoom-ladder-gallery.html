The zoom ladder

A proposal for what a node card shows at each zoom level, and five things it would remove. Nothing here is built. This page exists so you can veto before any code is written.

These are HTML mock-ups, not the React cards. The geometry, the type sizes, the icon sizes and the scaling arithmetic are copied from the real components at the SHA below, and every string is real starter copy — but a mock cannot prove what the product renders. Treat it as a picture of the proposal, not as evidence about the build.

The problem

The whole-model view is the least informative view we render

On a laptop, “Show whole model” parks a real starter between 0.26 and 0.38. There is exactly one rung in the product today — LABEL_LEGIBLE_ZOOM = 0.5 — and below it the card body hides and every per-node action unmounts at the same instant. So the view you reach for to understand the shape of the model is the one that tells you least about it.

The second half of the problem is a size rule, not a missing threshold. Canvas text counter-scales as you zoom out, so it stays roughly readable; the marks — the type glyph, the science icons, the action buttons, the connector dot — are fixed pixels and do not. At the 0.5 settle every mark on the card renders at half its design size while the text beside it renders at full size. That is why the icons look wrong before anything has unmounted.

The proposal is three rungs instead of one, and marks that counter-scale like text does.

The five things this would remove or change

Each is a separate veto. You can keep any of them and the ladder still stands.

  • V1Science icons disappear at the line rung — the guidance marks stop rendering below 0.5.
  • V2The double Sparkles — six cards across the five starters draw the same AI glyph twice.
  • V3Confirm becomes hover-only at full — it is always-on furniture today.
  • V4Per-node actions move to a screen-space toolbar from quiet down.
  • V5The bottom connector dot counter-scales instead of shrinking to a speck.

Section B

The five veto questions

Each one shows what changes on screen, a one-line recommendation, and a box to tick.

V1

Science icons are absent at the line rung

They already are today — the proposal keeps it that way rather than restoring them.

The science icons are the small guidance marks in the card’s top-right — “Olumi estimated this value”, “this range is unusually wide”, and so on. They are 12px fixed, and today they unmount at 0.5 along with everything else, so at the whole-model view they are already gone. The question is whether to bring them back. The arithmetic says no: were they kept at 0.30 they would render at 3.6 apparent pixels — present, unreadable, and enough to smear the corner of every card into grey noise. The proposal keeps them absent below 0.5 and spends the space on the number instead.

What you lose

At the whole-model view you can no longer see which cards carry a caution. You would have to zoom past 0.5 to find them. Nothing is deleted from the card — only from this rung.

What you gain

The one line of content per card becomes the only thing competing for the eye, which is the whole point of the rung.

Recommendation: CUT at line, and add a single count instead. If a model has cautions, say so once on the canvas — “7 cards need review” — rather than seven 3.6-pixel smudges.
KEEP the icons at line CUT them

Currently absent below 0.5, so KEEP is the change.

V2

The double Sparkles

A verified duplicate, not a proposal — the question is only whether you want it fixed here.

On a factor that Olumi inferred and nobody has reviewed, the header draws the Sparkles glyph twice: once as the value-provenance mark (valueProvenanceIcon.ts maps ai → Sparkles) and once as the olumi-estimate science icon (useScienceIcons.ts). Both say the same thing — “Olumi estimated this” — and they sit side by side.

Counted at this SHA: 20 of the 87 cards across the five starters — build-vs-buy 5 of 19, headcount-allocation 2 of 16, market-entry 4 of 18, pricing-model 3 of 15, vendor-selection 6 of 19. Roughly one card in four, and it is on every starter.

⚠ This page first said 6 of 87, and named the wrong card. The count was taken from a top-level extractionType field that nothing reads: useScienceIcons.ts:47 reads observedState?.extractionType, and the draft mapper never bridges the two. So the original worked example — Vendor Licensing Cost — has no observed_state and draws only ONE Sparkles. Found in review and re-derived independently from the five draft files. The correction makes the case stronger, not weaker: the collision is about three times more common than stated.

Why it matters more as you zoom out

Two 12px marks in a header that is 200px wide is a rounding error at full zoom. At the quiet rung it is two of the four things still legible in that corner, and it makes the card look like it carries two distinct cautions when it carries one.

Recommendation: CUT the duplicate — drop the olumi-estimate science icon when the provenance mark is already Sparkles. Keep the provenance mark: it is the one with the tooltip that names the value.
KEEP both CUT the duplicate

Affects 6 cards in the shipped starters; more on real drafted models.

V3

Confirm becomes hover-only at full

This is the “never a fixed row of furniture” rule applied to the one control that breaks it.

The three quick actions in the bottom-right are already hover-only — they render at opacity-0 until you hover, focus, or select the card. Confirm is not. It sits in the bottom-left, permanently visible on every inferred factor whose value is shown (ActionIcons.tsx). On a freshly drafted model that is a green tick on a large share of the canvas, at rest, saying nothing that the Sparkles mark has not already said.

The argument for keeping it

Confirming inferred values is the single highest-value thing a user can do to a fresh draft, and a control nobody can see is a control nobody uses. Hiding it may quietly reduce how often values get reviewed.

Recommendation: KEEP it visible, but only on cards that are actually unreviewed, and count it once on the canvas. This is the one place where furniture is earning its keep — the fix is a canvas-level “12 values need confirming” affordance, not hiding the tick.
KEEP Confirm always-on CUT to hover-only

This is the one of the five where I would not take my own default.

V4

Per-node actions move to a screen-space toolbar from quiet down

Today the actions unmount at 0.5 — the same instant the body hides — and there is nothing in their place. The proposal splits that: the icons stop being drawn on the card at 0.714, and from there down the actions for the selected node appear in a small toolbar that floats in screen space, at full size, regardless of zoom. You keep the actions at every rung; they simply stop being 11px marks scaled to 3px.

The trade

A screen-space toolbar is always legible and always in the same place, but it is one step further from the card — you select, then act, rather than acting directly. At full the on-card icons stay, so the direct gesture is unchanged where it works.

Recommendation: CUT the on-card icons below 0.714 and adopt the toolbar. Below 0.714 an 11px icon is under 8 apparent pixels — smaller than the pointer tip that has to hit it.
KEEP on-card icons at all rungs CUT to a screen-space toolbar
V5

The bottom connector dot counter-scales instead of shrinking to a speck

Corrected: the dot does not fade out today. It shrinks.

The card’s outgoing connector is a 12px dot with a 2px white ring, fixed (BaseNode.tsx:1050–1059). At 0.26 that is 3.1 apparent pixels with a half-pixel ring — it stops reading as a connection point and starts reading as a rendering artefact, which is a good part of why the whole-model view looks noisy. The proposal counter-scales it like text, so it holds roughly its design size on screen and stays the thing you drag from.

The trade

A counter-scaled dot is proportionally larger relative to a small card, so at the line rung it is visually heavier against a card that is deliberately quiet. The alternative is to counter-scale it only down to 0.5 and then let it fade — but a fade removes the drag target, which is worse.

Recommendation: CUT the fixed size — counter-scale, capped, and let the edge do the work of showing structure at the line rung.
KEEP the fixed 12px dot CUT to a counter-scaled dot

Engineering detail — constants, derivations, and three corrections

Where the numbers come from

The 0.714 rung is not a new magic number. It is the ratio of two quantities the design system already states: the canvas text floor and the canvas badge icon size.

QuantityValueSource
Canvas text floor10pxDESIGN_SYSTEM.md — canvas contexts use 10–12px; edgeLabel is 10px
Canvas badge icon14pxDESIGN_SYSTEM.md — “canvas node badge / panel inline · 14px · w-3.5 h-3.5”
ICON_LEGIBLE_ZOOM10 ÷ 14 ≈ 0.714derived — an icon is legible while it renders at or above the text floor
LABEL_LEGIBLE_ZOOM0.5src/canvas/utils/zoomLegibility.ts:75 — the existing rung, unchanged

0.4 is not a rung. It is where the counter-scaled title crosses the floor: the label scale is capped at 2, so a 12px title holds 10px apparent down to 10 ÷ (12 × 2) ≈ 0.4167, and below that it degrades. That is a consequence of the cap, not a threshold to switch on.

The scaling arithmetic, and why the mock reproduces the capture

Canvas text carries --canvas-label-scale, which is min(2, 1/z). This page uses the same expression, so the apparent sizes it renders match the deployed measurements exactly:

Measured on stagingValueReproduced here as
min title px at z = 0.512.0012 × 2 × 0.5 = 12.00
min title px at z = 0.25956.2312 × 2 × 0.2595 = 6.23
min LOD-line px at z = 0.25955.7111 × 2 × 0.2595 = 5.71

Marks carry no such variable. In this page --ms is 1 on every TODAY card (fixed px, the current behaviour) and min(2, 1/z) on every PROPOSED card. That single difference is the whole “why the icons look wrong” finding, rendered.

Correction 1 — V5’s premise was wrong

The brief for this gallery said the bottom connector dot goes opacity-0 at the line rung. It does not. At this SHA the source Handle is rendered unconditionally at 12×12 with a 2px white border and no opacity rule (BaseNode.tsx:1050–1059). The only opacity-0 in the canvas node tree is the quick-action row (NodeQuickActions.tsx:301), which is hover, focus, selection and coarse-pointer gated. V5 is restated above as the fixed-size problem, which is real.

Correction 2 — the quick actions are already hover-only

“A fixed row of furniture” is not quite what ships. The three quick actions render at opacity-0 and appear on hover, on focus-within, on selection (alwaysVisible={selected}, BaseNode.tsx:610) and always on coarse pointers. The control that is unconditional furniture is Confirm, which is why V3 is about Confirm and not about the row.

Correction 3 — at exactly 0.5 nothing has changed yet, and that is the gap

isLodZoom is strict, so at z = 0.5 the LOD state is off. The deployed capture confirms it: at the 0.5 settle, lodActive: false, bodyHidden: 0 — and noLodLine: 19, i.e. all nineteen cards carry no reduced line. So 0.5 is the full card at half mark-size with no fallback line. That is precisely the band the proposed quiet rung exists to fix.

The double-Sparkles derivation

Both glyphs fire on the same condition. valueProvenance.ts:316 maps provenance: "ai_inferred" to { kind: 'ai' }, and valueProvenanceIcon.ts:75 maps ai → Sparkles. Separately useScienceIcons.ts:76–84 pushes an olumi-estimate icon — also Sparkles — when extractionType === 'inferred' && !isReviewedSource(source). ⚠ Note the field it reads: observedState?.extractionType (useScienceIcons.ts:47), not the top-level extractionType, which nothing consumes. Counting nodes in the five starter drafts that satisfy both: 20 of 87 — build-vs-buy 5/19, headcount-allocation 2/16, market-entry 4/18, pricing-model 3/15, vendor-selection 6/19.

Scope

This page is the only file in its pull request. It changes no product behaviour, adds no constant, and touches nothing under src/ — another lane owns src/canvas/**. If the ladder is approved, the constants in the first table are what a build lane would add.
