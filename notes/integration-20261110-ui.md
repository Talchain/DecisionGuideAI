## Decisions I made that you may want to overrule

- Retry lock. A changed selection is refused only after an approval whose answer never came (a dropped connection or a server error). After a definite refusal from the engine the person may change the choices and approve again.
- A proposal the engine marks as not supported has no Approve, so it offers no reading choice.
- A card's ask that fronts Olumi while the chat floats: the window keeps its place and takes focus, and the dock goes back to its next tab. A press on the dock's Olumi tab brings the chat back to the dock.
- A dragged window is held inside the free canvas; it can no longer be dragged over the dock. A window opened shorter keeps that height when dragged.
- The approver line reads "Approving records <name> as the person who approved; the words Olumi read stay their writer's", using the View's `you`.
- A lint in this repo forbids `.filter(` in ViewChat.tsx; I used the file's existing flatMap idiom for the chosen ids.

An independent review of the diff (four reviewers, each finding re-checked) found the first three points plus one test row that proved nothing. All are fixed in the commit.
